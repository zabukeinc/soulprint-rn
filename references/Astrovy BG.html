✓ Photography Style
  • · Moody, atmospheric night skies
  • · Soft bokeh and dreamy depth of field
  • · People looking upward, inward, exploring
  • · Natural light, candlelight, starlight
  • · Tinted with brand violet overlay when needed
✗ Avoid
  • · Bright, harsh studio lighting
  • · Generic stock photo aesthetics
  • · Cluttered, busy compositions
  • · Unrelated color palettes (yellows, reds)
  • · Posed, corporate-feeling photos
